Submission Requirements
Authors are required to prepare and submit the extended abstract in accordance with the following requirements:
The extended abstract shall not exceed four (4) pages, including figures, tables and references. 
The manuscript shall be prepared on A4-sized paper using single-line spacing. 
The manuscript shall be typed in Times New Roman font. 
The page margins shall be set at 2.54 cm (1 inch) on all sides. 
The manuscript shall be submitted in Portable Document Format (PDF). 
Figures, tables and illustrations shall be clearly numbered, captioned and referred to within the text. 
Authors who use Artificial Intelligence (AI) tools during the development of the innovation or preparation of the manuscript shall disclose the AI tools used and describe how the outputs were verified and validated by the authors.
The submitted extended abstract must be the original work of the author(s) and must not infringe any copyright or intellectual property rights.
Failure to comply with these submission requirements may result in the extended abstract being rejected or returned for correction.

1. Background / Problem Statement
Briefly describe the real problem, institutional or industry context, target users, and why the issue requires an innovative intervention. Include evidence of need where possible. Clearly explain the gap between current practice and the proposed innovation.
2. Innovation Objective
State the main objective and specific objectives of the innovation. Objectives should be measurable and aligned with the problem statement.
3. Description of Innovation
Explain the product/innovation, method, process, model, module, application, teaching aid, system, log prompt or service developed. 
4. Methodology / Development Process
Describe how the innovation was developed. Explain the development process, methods, tools, technologies or platforms used, and how the innovation was tested or validated. Include user feedback, pilot implementation, or evaluation results where relevant. You may also include a simple workflow, conceptual framework, system architecture, or process diagram to support the explanation.

References 
In-text citations shall follow the author–year citation format (e.g., Author, 2006; Author & Author, 2007; Author et al., 2008). 
References shall be listed alphabetically at the end of the manuscript. 
Authors are encouraged to use the APA (7th Edition) referencing style consistently throughout the manuscript.
